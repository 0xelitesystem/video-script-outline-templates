# Reaction outline

**Length:** Variable (depends on the source content length)
**Goal:** Add value beyond just watching the source content; commentary that's worth the viewer's extra time

## Important first

Reaction videos have specific copyright and fair-use considerations. Different platforms enforce differently. This outline assumes:

- You're commenting on, criticizing, or analyzing the source content (transformative use)
- You're not republishing the source content as the primary value
- You have the right or fair-use basis to show the clips you're showing
- You'll comply with platform-specific reaction guidelines (YouTube has explicit ones)

## 0:15 - 0:45 - Setup

- What the source content is
- What you know going in (have you seen related content, do you know the creator)
- What you're listening for / looking out for

## 0:45 - body - The reaction

For each segment of the source:

### [Segment topic / timestamp in source]

- Show the source segment (legally, see "Important first" above)
- Pause and react
- Add commentary, analysis, or contrasting perspective
- Skip ahead if a segment doesn't justify reaction

[Don't react to EVERY second of the source. Pick segments worth your audience's time.]

## (~85% mark) - Overall response

- What you took away
- Whether you'd recommend the source
- What you'd want to see more or less of

## Notes for reaction format

- **Add value or don't post.** A reaction that's just "wow, cool, yeah" is content theft with extra steps.
- **Show meaningful engagement.** Quote, paraphrase, contrast, build on. Demonstrate you're processing, not just watching.
- **Skip strategically.** Show enough source to make your reaction make sense; skip past content that doesn't add to your reaction.
- **Credit the source prominently.** Channel name, video title, link in description. Pinned comment.
- **Read the platform's reaction policy** before publishing. Policies change.
