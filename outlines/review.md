# Review (single product) outline

**Length:** 8-15 minutes
**Goal:** Viewer leaves knowing whether to buy

## 0:00 - 0:15 - Hook

[State what you're reviewing and the headline verdict. "I used X for 30 days and here's what I think." Or: "X is the most overrated product of 2026."]

## 0:15 - 0:45 - Promise + provenance

- What the product is (briefly)
- How you got it (bought, sample, gifted, sponsored)
- How long you used it for
- Your relevant experience with similar products

## 0:45 - 1:00 - Disclosure (mandatory)

[For reviews this is non-negotiable. Disclose how you got the product, payment if any, and any other material connection.]

See [product-review-honesty-guide](https://github.com/0xelitesystem/product-review-honesty-guide) for specific language.

## 1:00 - 2:00 - Specs / overview

- Price as of recording date
- Key specs / features (briefly, this is review, not unboxing)
- Who it's marketed to

## 2:00 - body - The actual review

### What works

- Specific, observed strengths
- Why these matter for the target user
- Demonstration when possible

### What doesn't

- Specific, observed weaknesses
- Severity (deal-breaker, annoyance, edge case)
- Whether the brand has acknowledged or fixed similar issues

### Surprises (good or bad)

- Things you didn't expect from the marketing
- Things that came up only after sustained use

### Comparison context

- How it compares to the obvious alternative
- Where it fits in the price tier

## (~80% mark) - Verdict

- **Buy if:** [specific situations]
- **Skip if:** [specific situations]
- **Wait for:** [if there's a known update or competitor coming]

## (~85% mark) - Disclosure recap (especially for sponsored/gifted)

[Short re-disclosure: "Reminder, X sent me this for review but did not pay for the video." Belt-and-suspenders.]

## Notes for review format

- **Negative observations build credibility.** A review with no criticisms looks like an ad.
- **Date-stamp your review.** Products change. "Reviewing as of [month/year]" lets viewers calibrate.
- **Be specific about your use case.** "I tested this for [specific workflow]" beats "I tried it out."
- **Update older reviews when the product changes significantly.** Either pin a comment or re-record.
