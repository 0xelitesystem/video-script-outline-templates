## Notes for comparison format

- **No fake balance.** If X is clearly better for most viewers, say so. False even-handedness is a credibility killer.
- **Acknowledge bias.** Comparing your own product vs a competitor? Disclose. Comparing two products you affiliate with at different commission rates? Disclose.
- **Show, don't list.** A side-by-side screen recording of the same task in both is worth 100 spec sheets.
- **Address the "well actually" comments before they happen.** "Yes, I know X also does Z, but only on the Pro tier." This earns trust.
