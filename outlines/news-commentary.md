# News / commentary outline

**Length:** 5-12 minutes
**Goal:** Viewer understands what happened, why it matters, and your perspective

## 0:00 - 0:15 - Hook

[Lead with the most newsworthy fact, not your opinion. Opinion comes after the facts. "[Company] just announced [thing]. Here's what's actually happening." Not: "[Company] is in trouble and here's why."]

## 0:15 - 0:45 - The facts

- What happened (when, where, who)
- Source links (in description, mention "sources in the description")
- Recency (when did this happen, when did you record)

## 0:45 - 1:30 - Context

- Background needed to understand the news
- Why this is different from past similar events (or how it's the same)

## 1:30 - body - Analysis / commentary

This is YOUR contribution. Make it useful:

### Why this matters

- Who is affected
- How significant is this on a [small/medium/large] scale
- What's the likely follow-on

### What the consensus narrative gets wrong

- The takes you've seen elsewhere
- Where they're missing context
- (Optional, only if defensible) where you think they're wrong

### What you're watching for next

- Specific events or signals that would change your view
- Timeline expectations

## (~85% mark) - Open question to audience

[A genuine question, not an engagement-bait one. "What would change YOUR mind on this?" works. "Smash that like if you agree" doesn't.]

## Notes for news / commentary format

- **Date-stamp prominently.** News videos age fast. "Recorded [date]" on screen helps viewers calibrate.
- **Cite sources.** Every claim should be traceable to a source in the description.
- **Distinguish facts from analysis.** "What happened: X. What I think: Y." Mixing them erodes trust.
- **Update if proven wrong.** Pinned comment or follow-up video. Ignoring corrections kills credibility faster than the original mistake.
- **Be careful with breaking news.** First takes age badly. If something is still developing, say so and revisit later.
