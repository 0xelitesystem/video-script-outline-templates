# Tried-it-for-N-days outline

**Length:** 10-15 minutes
**Goal:** Genuine first-person account of an extended experience

## 0:00 - 0:15 - Hook

[State the experiment specifically. "I used X for 30 days. Here's what happened." The word "happened" matters, implies the outcome wasn't pre-decided.]

## 0:15 - 0:45 - Setup

- What you were trying to find out
- Why you picked this product/method/approach
- Your starting state (skill level, situation, expectations)
- How long the test was

## 0:45 - 1:00 - Disclosure (if applicable)

## 1:00 - body - The experience

### Day 1 (or week 1)

- First impressions
- Initial wins
- Initial frustrations

### Mid-experiment

- What changed once novelty wore off
- Routine or habit you developed
- Surprise moments

### End of experiment

- Final state
- What you learned
- What you'd do differently

## (~85% mark) - The honest verdict

- **Would you keep doing it?** Yes/no, why
- **Who would benefit:** specific situations
- **Who shouldn't bother:** specific situations
- **What surprised you most**

## Notes for tried-it format

- **Document during the experiment, not after.** Memory fills in details that didn't happen.
- **Don't decide the outcome before you start.** "I tried X and it changed my life" videos that were obviously pre-scripted hit a credibility ceiling.
- **Show evidence.** Screenshots, photos, screen recordings from during the test. Without evidence it reads as fiction.
- **Acknowledge the experiment's limits.** N days isn't long enough to know X. Say so.
- **Be honest about quitting early.** If you quit on day 18 of a 30-day test, say so and explain why. Viewers respect honesty more than completion.
