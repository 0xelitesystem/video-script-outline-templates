# Short / vertical (60s) outline

**Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Goal:** One specific takeaway, delivered before viewers swipe

## 0:00 - 0:03 - Hook

[Three seconds. Maybe four. State the value or pose the curiosity gap immediately. Visual must be moving from frame 1.]

Examples:
- "Here's the one thing wrong with most [thing]."
- "[Surprising fact about thing]."
- "Try this for [outcome]."

## 0:03 - 0:10 - Promise

[What the viewer will know or be able to do by the end. Be specific. Vague promises lose Shorts viewers in seconds.]

## 0:10 - 0:50 - Body

ONE point. Not three. Not "I have 5 tips" stuffed into 60 seconds. Pick the strongest single insight and deliver it well.

For most Shorts, this means:

- The setup (3-5 seconds)
- The reveal/demo/explanation (20-30 seconds)
- The "so what" (5-10 seconds)

## 0:50 - 0:55 - Disclosure (if applicable)

[Even Shorts need disclosure when there's affiliate or sponsored content. On-screen text is usually best given the time pressure.]

## 0:55 - 0:60 - CTA

ONE thing:
- "Follow for more on [specific topic]"
- "Comment [specific thing] if you want the full version"

## Notes for Shorts format

- **First frame = visible value.** If your first frame is a face talking with no context, viewers swipe.
- **Captions on by default.** Most Shorts viewers watch muted.
- **No reused long-form opens.** "Hey what's up everyone" wastes 2 of your 60 seconds.
- **One Short = one idea.** If your idea needs more, it's a long-form video, not a Short.
- **Loops.** A Short that ends in a way that makes sense to immediately rewatch from the beginning gets 2x watch time.
