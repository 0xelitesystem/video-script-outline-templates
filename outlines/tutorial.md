# Tutorial / how-to outline

**Length:** 8-20 minutes
**Goal:** Viewer can do [the thing] by the end of the video

## 0:00 - 0:15 - Hook

[State the specific problem this tutorial solves. Be concrete. "How to set up X to do Y in under 10 minutes" beats "Today I'll show you something cool."]

## 0:15 - 0:45 - Promise + prerequisites

- What you'll be able to do by the end
- What you need before starting (software, accounts, hardware, prerequisite knowledge)
- Realistic time estimate

## 0:45 - 1:30 - Disclosure (if applicable) + intro

[Disclosure first if affiliate/sponsored. Then channel intro IF you do one. Many channels skip the intro because it kills retention.]

## 1:30 - end of body - Steps

For each step:

### Step N: [Verb-led name, e.g. "Install the X plugin"]

- What we're doing in this step
- Show, don't tell (screen recording or close-up shot)
- Common mistake / gotcha
- How to verify the step worked before moving on

[Repeat for each step. Aim for 3-7 major steps. If you have more, you may need to split into multiple videos or use chapters.]

## (~85% mark) - Wrap-up

- Recap what was accomplished
- Show the working result
- Common follow-up questions and where to find answers

## CTA (one)

[ONE specific ask. Not "subscribe and like and comment and join the patreon and check out my course." Pick the most important.]

## Outro

- What to watch next (related video on your channel)
- Sign-off

## Notes for tutorial format

- **Show your screen / hands within the first 30 seconds.** Viewers want to verify they're in the right place.
- **Number your steps.** Adds chapter markers, helps viewers find their lost spot.
- **Anticipate errors.** "If you see [error], check [thing]." Saves you from comments.
- **Test the tutorial yourself end-to-end on a fresh setup.** Most "doesn't work" comments are from skipped steps in a recording done on a pre-configured machine.
