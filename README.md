# video-script-outline-templates

Structural skeletons for common video formats. Outline-level only, the bones, not the script.

## Why this exists

Most "viral script" repos online are clickbait engines pretending to be templates. This isn't that. These are honest structural outlines for common video formats: where the hook goes, where the proof goes, where the CTA goes, where the disclosure goes. You bring the actual content.

If you're starting a script and staring at a blank page, pick the closest format here, paste the outline into your editor, and replace each section header with your actual content.

## How to use an outline

1. Open the outline for your format
2. Copy it into your script doc
3. Replace each `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER]` with your actual content
4. Delete sections that don't apply
5. Read the whole script aloud once before recording, pacing is everything

## What outlines do not give you

- **Hooks.** Your hook depends on what's actually compelling about YOUR content. Templates can't write that for you. The outline shows WHERE the hook goes (first 5-10 seconds) and what role it plays (curiosity gap or clear promise of value); the words are yours.
- **Voice.** Your voice is what makes the video yours. Outlines preserve a structure that works; voice is layered on top.
- **Proof / examples.** Your specific examples come from your work, your tests, your experience. The outline shows where they fit.
- **Specific calls-to-action.** What you ask viewers to do depends on your goals.

## Sections common to all outlines

Most outlines include:

- **Hook (0:00-0:15)**, give viewers a reason to keep watching
- **Promise / preview**, what they'll know or be able to do by the end
- **Disclosure**, when applicable, BEFORE the recommendation
- **Body**, the actual content (varies by format)
- **CTA**, single, clear ask
- **Outro**, what's next

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Putting the CTA before delivering value
- Making promises in the hook the rest of the video doesn't keep
- Skipping the disclosure when there's affiliate / sponsored content (see [affiliate-disclosure-templates](https://github.com/0xelitesystem/affiliate-disclosure-templates))
- Writing every section to the same length (rhythm needs variation)
- Adding 3 CTAs to one video (one per video; viewers do one thing or zero things)
